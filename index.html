Loan Risk Prediction Analysis

Explored 614 loan applications — average applicant income of $5,403 with high-income outliers up to $81,000, ~78% of applicants Graduates, and Semiurban properties the most common category (233 cases) — then compared two classification models to predict approval, weighing accuracy against the recall needed to catch risky approvals.

Python Scikit-Learn Pandas Logistic Regression Random Forest
View on GitHub →
  • Credit History emerged as the strongest predictor — applicants meeting guidelines saw a significantly higher approval probability
  • Baseline approval rate of 68% (422 of 614 applications) provided a balanced foundation for the model
  • Logistic Regression: 85.06% accuracy with 97% recall on approved loans — the model taken forward
  • Random Forest: 81.82% accuracy, but a stronger 56% recall on denied cases, better at catching subtle risk patterns
